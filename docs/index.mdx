## Introduction

In any organization, especially those managing critical systems, it's important to control who can access which servers—and to keep a record of what they do once they're in. Our server access management system makes this easy by automating user access, keeping everything up-to-date through a GitHub repository, and sending real-time notifications when important actions happen.

The system is made up of two lightweight and easy-to-understand tools: **Keyhouse** and **Watchdog**. Together, they help your team manage access smoothly, securely, and with full visibility into all server activity. Whether it's onboarding new team members or auditing server usage, everything is handled in a clean, automated way.

## Overview

### 1. Keyhouse: A central database for easy access control

Keyhouse is the central database that allows access control. It stores SSH keys of all members in the `keys/` folder. Access rules for different servers and projects are organized in the `hosts/` folder.

Whenever access needs to change (for example, when someone joins or leaves a project), updates are made here and synced automatically.

### 2. Watchdog: Access Syncing and Real-Time Privilege Monitoring

Watchdog is a Rust binary installed on each server. It keeps the server's users and groups in sync with Keyhouse — either automatically on every SSH login, or periodically via a scheduled `watchdog update` — by polling the Keyhouse repository and applying commit diffs through the [`watchdog_utils_II`](https://github.com/sdslabs/watchdog-utils) crate.

It also keeps an eye on what's happening on your servers. It watches for key actions like people logging in via SSH, users running sudo commands, and anyone switching users using `su`.

When any of these events occur, Watchdog sends real-time alerts to a **Slack channel**, helping teams stay informed and ensuring there's a clear record of privileged activity.

---

Together, these two tools give you full control over who can access your servers, what they can do, and when they do it — with everything being tracked and updated automatically. Simple to use and easy to maintain.
